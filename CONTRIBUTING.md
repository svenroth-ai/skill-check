# Contributing

Thanks for looking. Skill Check is small on purpose.

- **Found a bug or a wrong result?** Open an issue with what you expected, what you saw, and your operating system. Please do not paste your skills if they are private; a short made-up example is enough.
- **Want to change something?** Open an issue first so we can agree on the idea. Small fixes can go straight to a pull request.
- **Run the tests** before you send anything: `python -m unittest discover -s tests`. A fix comes with a test that fails without it.
- **Words matter.** The view is written for people who are not technical. Plain words, US English, no jargon, and no verdicts about other people's tools.
- **No network, no uploads.** Skill Check stays local. A change that sends data anywhere will not be merged.

By contributing you agree that your work is released under the MIT license.
